## Captions
- **Font:** Montserrat (Bold base, Extrabold emphasis, Black payoff)
- **Look:** lowercase, white, 2 to 3 words per caption, about 56% down the frame

## Content
- **Niche:**
- **Hooks:** bold or contrarian claim, or a number or result. Several hook variants per clip for trial reels.
- **Endings:** always pay off what the hook promised.
- **Never:** open on "so" or "um"; end mid-thought; identify a client.

## Editing
- **Length:** 35 to 55s per clip
- **Pacing:** tight, without choppy cuts mid-sentence
- **Music:** none
- **Captions show what you meant:** fix small slips of tense or grammar, never the meaning.

## Graphics
Off unless asked for. Colors and font live in `brand/graphics.json`.
