01 · The idea

Direct the edit. Don't do it.

Vibe editing means you work like a director. You say what you want, Claude Code runs the tools, and you review the result. There's no timeline to learn.

The kit isn't an app. It's a folder of written rules and scripts: what makes a good clip, how to cut it, how captions should look, and what has to be true before anything counts as finished. Claude Code follows those rules on your computer.

Your taste is the input

Hooks, endings, things a clip must never do. You state them once in the brand interview, and every edit reads them.

Rules beat prompts

When something comes out wrong, the fix goes into the rules, so the next job starts from it.

Checks over hope

Scripts measure the output. Claude also re-listens to each clip and looks at its frames before showing you.

Your footage is safe

Source files are referenced in place, never changed. Everything new goes into two folders beside them.

Two folders do all the work. 10_WORK holds the scratch files. 20_DELIVER holds what you post.

02 · The vertical edit

From footage to finished clip

Open a folder of footage in Claude Code and say "vibe edit this folder."

  1. 1

    Inventory

    Check every video and look at contact sheets: what is it, who's on camera, what's a test clip.
  2. 2

    Transcribe

    Get every word with its timing and map the silences. Everything is cut from this.
  3. 3

    Decide what each file is

    A stack of hooks, a scripted read or a call. Each has its own way of being cut.
  4. 4

    Pick the clips

    Find a hook and a payoff, with no filler at the start and no ending mid-thought. On calls, use only your own voice and never identify a client.
  5. 5

    Cut and caption

    Snap cuts to words, trim dead air, burn in captions, level the voice.
  6. 6

    Check

    Re-transcribe what was delivered, run the quality script, look at the frames, read the burned captions.
  7. 7

    Deliver

    Clips plus notes: hooks, which variants pair up, and where a proof screenshot would fit.
One recording, many tests. Read several hooks, then one body. Each hook plus the body becomes its own clip, ready to run as a trial reel.

03 · Optional graphics

Only when a clip needs them

A clean clip with captions is finished, and many good reels have no graphics. If you want them, say "add graphics to this clip." When a graphic plays, you drop into a card at the bottom and the graphic takes the top. When it ends, you're back to full frame. Your head stays in frame because the kit scans for your face first.

When you sayIt shows
A numberA big figure that counts up
A listRows that appear as you name them, or stay blurred until you do
A contrastTwo cards side by side
StepsA chain, one step after another
A cycleA ring that draws itself
Before and afterBars that grow
ProofYour real screenshot, uncropped
The askAn end card with a button
Tease, then reveal. Put your three points up blurred, then un-blur each as you say it. Viewers can count them, so they stay to find out what they are.

05 · Get running

Set up in 15 minutes

  1. 1

    Get Claude Code

    Download the desktop app and sign in. You need a paid Claude plan.
  2. 2

    Open this folder

    Open the kit folder in Claude Code and paste the box from ONBOARDING.md.
  3. 3

    Answer the questions

    Claude installs what it needs, then interviews you about your brand.
  4. 4

    Make a test clip

    Give it one short vertical video. Say what you'd change. Repeat until you like it.
